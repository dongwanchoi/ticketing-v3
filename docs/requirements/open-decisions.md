## Hold TTL and per-hold size (approved 2026-10-29 KST)
User explicitly approved TTL 5 minutes and maximum 4 seats per hold in chat. Alternatives considered: 3/10 minutes and 1/6 seats. Short TTL reduces inventory blocking but increases checkout expiry. Expiry is computed server-side in UTC. This does not approve cancellation, resale, retry, role transitions or unlimited concurrent holds per user. Aggregate active-hold policy still needs clarification before anti-abuse behavior is finalized. No policy imported from V2.
Also pending: full/partial cancellation, resale after cancellation, payment retry after failure, role transitions, seat grading and deployment costs. Recommend full cancellation only for initial scope, but do not implement until approved. Core integrity and authorization changes require review.
